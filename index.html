MAREK SALAT

PRODUCER // MIX ENGINEER // ARTIST

Marek Salat is an audio producer, mix engineer and independent artist based in Vancouver. From an audiophile at an early age, to experimentation with sample-based production in the mid 2010’s, to 2019 alum of Nimbus School of Recording and Media, Marek’s journey through music has helped form a uniquely well-rounded approach to audio production. This is clearly demonstrated in his appreciation for analog, acoustic and digital sound techniques, and provides his clients many options to realize their vision.
